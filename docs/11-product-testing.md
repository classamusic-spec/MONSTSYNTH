# 11 · Product test plan (behavioural)

Children are **observed, not interviewed**. We don't ask "was it easy?"; we watch what hands and faces do.

## Setup

* 8–12 children per band (3–5 and 6–10), tablet held sideways or on a table, a parent nearby.
* Fresh install, Little Monster mode for 3–5, Monster Maker for 6–10. Hints on for half the sessions, off for the other half.
* Facilitator says only: *"This is Monster Synth. You can play with it however you like."* No instructions, no pointing. Help only if the child asks twice.
* 12 minutes free play, then *"Can you play me your song?"*

## What to record

| Signal | How it is observed / measured | Good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Time to first sound | stopwatch from app open | ≤ 5 s |
| Do they know monsters can be touched? | first touch on a monster, unprompted | within the first minute |
| Can they make a loop without explanation? | Record used and a loop plays back | ≥ 60 % (3–5), ≥ 85 % (6–10) |
| Can they find Play and Record? | taps on ▶ and ● within the session | both, unprompted |
| Do they add a second layer? | a second monster gets a loop | ≥ 50 % (3–5) |
| Repeated exploration | the same interaction ≥ 5 times in a row | noted per interaction |
| Delight | laughing, "look!", showing a parent, dancing | noted with timestamp |
| Ignored controls | never touched in 12 min | candidates to remove |
| Frustration | repeated taps with no effect, "it's not working", giving the tablet away | every instance investigated |
| Returning to an earlier idea | opens a previous song from the shelf, or undoes to recover | noted |
| Ownership | says "my song", names it, plays it for someone | the goal of the product |

## Specific questions for 0.1

1. Does the sleeping-monster wake screen read as "tap me", or do children wait?
2. Does the first-note downbeat (Record then play) feel natural, or do children expect a count-in?
3. Is the loop badge (sleep) understood? Is hold-to-clear ever found?
4. Do children discover squish gestures on the monsters, or only use keys?
5. Do they use Blocks without a hint? Does Magic feel like "their" song or the app's?
6. Is Mimic the favourite (expected), and is the chipmunk effect delightful or confusing?
7. Portrait vs landscape: do children hold phones sideways without the rotate screen?

## After each round

1. List the three biggest frustrations and the three biggest delights.
2. **Simplify first**: remove or hide ignored controls before adding anything new.
3. Adjust Monster Magic parameters (quantize strength, loop length, auto-stop) from what children actually played.
4. Re-run the automated checks and the level check before the next round.
